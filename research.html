Research themes

At temperatures above ~150 MeV, nuclear matter undergoes a crossover transition into a deconfined state of quarks and gluons — the Quark-Gluon Plasma. Our group develops theoretical tools and multi-stage numerical simulations to extract the fundamental properties of this matter from experimental observables.

  1. 1

    Initial conditions & pre-equilibrium dynamics

    The geometry and event-by-event quantum fluctuations of the nuclear overlap region at the moment of impact seed all subsequent collective dynamics. We investigate how pre-hydrodynamic evolution transforms these initial conditions into the energy-momentum tensor that initializes relativistic fluid dynamics.

  2. 2

    Relativistic viscous hydrodynamics

    We develop and apply (3+1)D viscous relativistic fluid dynamics to describe the macroscopic expansion of the QGP. Our work examines causality constraints in nonlinear hydrodynamic simulations, shear and bulk transport coefficients, and species-dependent viscous corrections at particlization.

  3. 3

    Scaled transverse-momentum spectra

    We identified that the shape of transverse-momentum (pT) spectra exhibits remarkable universality across collision systems and centralities when scaled by the mean transverse momentum. This provides a sensitive probe of thermalization, radial flow, and the onset of collectivity from small to large collision systems.

  4. 4

    Anisotropic flow & collective correlations

    Anisotropic flow harmonics (vn), momentum-dependent flow fluctuations, and principal-component analyses carry detailed information about initial-state geometry and QGP viscosity. We design new observables and perform precision comparisons with LHC and RHIC measurements.

  5. 5

    Nuclear structure in high-energy collisions

    Relativistic collisions take instantaneous snapshots of the colliding nuclei. We study how nuclear quadrupole and octupole deformations, neutron skins, short-range nucleon-nucleon correlations, and light-ion cluster geometries imprint themselves on final-state observables.

  6. 6

    Bayesian inference & machine learning

    Using full hybrid simulation chains (TRENTo / IP-Glasma → KoMPoST → MUSIC → iSS → UrQMD), Gaussian process emulators, and constrained functional priors, we perform rigorous Bayesian parameter estimation to quantify uncertainties in the QGP equation of state and transport coefficients.

Software & simulation codes

Our group contributes to and utilizes open-source simulation codes for heavy-ion phenomenology:

MUSIC Relativistic hydrodynamics

(3+1)D relativistic viscous hydrodynamics simulation code solving energy-momentum and charge conservation with shear and bulk dissipation.

Isobar-Sampler Nuclear geometry

Monte Carlo nuclear configuration generator for sampling nucleon positions with deformation and short-range correlation effects.

iEBE-MUSIC Hybrid event generator

Integrated event-by-event simulation framework coupling initial conditions, pre-equilibrium evolution, MUSIC hydrodynamics, iSS particlization, and UrQMD.

ExTrEMe Pipeline Multi-stage chain

Full documentation of the TRENTo → KoMPoST → MUSIC → iSS → UrQMD simulation chain used across our joint projects with the ExTrEMe Collaboration.
